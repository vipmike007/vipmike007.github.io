About关于我

Engineer turned customer advocate从工程师到客户的技术伙伴

Deep hands-on engineering roots, now applied to helping large enterprises get the most out of open source.深厚的一线工程背景，如今用于帮助大型企业充分发挥开源技术的价值。

I started my career testing virtualization products and spent five years at Red Hat building and leading the virtio-win quality engineering team from scratch, getting Windows para-virtualized drivers certified by Microsoft (WHQL / SVVP).

Since then I've delivered private and hybrid cloud solutions on HPE Helion OpenStack and CloudSystem, kept Oracle's global demo cloud running across 9 data centers, and now work as a Principal Technical Account Manager at Red Hat for strategic customers in China.

To pair technical depth with management skills, I earned a Master of Engineering Management from Beijing University of Chemical Technology in 2024 and graduated as an Outstanding Graduate.

此后，我基于 HPE Helion OpenStack 和 CloudSystem 交付私有云与混合云方案，保障甲骨文覆盖 9 个数据中心的全球演示云平台稳定运行；目前在红帽担任首席技术客户经理，服务中国区战略客户。
