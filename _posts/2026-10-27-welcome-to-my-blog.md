<div class="en" markdown="1">

After more than fifteen years working with Linux, virtualization, and cloud — from testing qemu-kvm
at Red Hat, to delivering OpenStack clouds at HPE, to helping strategic customers as a Technical
Account Manager — I've decided it's time to start writing things down.

## What I'll write about

- **Cloud & virtualization** — OpenShift, OpenStack, KVM, and RHEL in real customer environments
- **Customer success** — what I've learned about being a trusted technical advisor
- **Engineering management** — ideas from my MEM studies and from leading teams

## Why write?

> The best way to learn something is to explain it to someone else.

Writing helps me organize what I've learned, and I hope some of it will be useful to you too.

Thanks for stopping by — feel free to [reach out](/#contact) anytime.
